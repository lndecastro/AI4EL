AI for Everyday Life#

Welcome to the AI for Everyday Life Program (AI4EL), part of the FGCU AI Academy led by the Dendritic Institute for Human-Centered AI & Data Science at Florida Gulf Coast University.

The AI4EL Program is the practical, applied offering of the FGCU AI Academy, designed for learners of all backgrounds who want to use Artificial Intelligence tools confidently in their daily and professional lives. This program was designed to require no technical background and also be tool agnostic. Positioned alongside the AI Literacy Program (which builds conceptual and historical foundations), the AI4EL Program focuses on a different, equally essential question: How do I actually use AI to get things done? Through hands-on practice, real-world demonstrations, and a human-centered philosophy, participants move from curiosity to capability, learning to draft emails, generate images, create presentations, plan tasks, and much more, always with critical thinking and responsible use at the core.

This Jupyter Book is designed to be your practical companion through the world of AI tools, from understanding what AI is and how to talk to it, to creating rich multimedia content and building habits that last.

Program Goals#

The goal of this program is to equip everyday learners with the confidence, skills, and mindset to use AI tools effectively and responsibly in their personal and professional lives. By the end of this journey, you will:

  • Understand what AI is, and what it is not, in plain, jargon-free language.

  • Know how to communicate with AI tools to get useful, high-quality results.

  • Use AI to complete real tasks: writing emails, summarizing documents, creating spreadsheets, generating images, building presentations, and much more.

  • Recognize when to trust AI outputs, and when to question them.

  • Build a personal AI toolkit and a set of habits that you can apply immediately.

This program is for everyone, whether you have never used an AI tool before or you have experimented a little and want to go further with confidence.

Learning Objectives#

By completing this program, you will be able to:

  • Explain what AI is and describe how it works in everyday terms, without technical jargon.

  • Use prompt engineering techniques to get better results from AI tools.

  • Apply AI to written communication: drafting and improving emails, documents, and reports.

  • Use AI to create, analyze and simplify spreadsheets.

  • Use AI to create a personal professional page and a tailored CV and cover letter from your LinkedIn profile.

  • Create AI-generated images for personal and professional use.

  • Build AI-assisted presentations faster and more effectively.

  • Design a digital event invitation as an interactive webpage using AI.

  • Plan the core components of a company or organization with AI, then build its brand identity and website.

  • Experiment with AI music and avatar tools for content creation.

  • Apply a verification-first mindset to evaluate AI outputs critically.

  • Protect your privacy and data when using AI tools.

  • Design a personal AI action plan with habits you can start using right away.

How to Use This Jupyter Book#

  • Navigate the classes using the left-hand sidebar or the Next/Previous buttons at the bottom of each page.

  • Download pages as .md or .pdf for your notes and reference materials.

  • Look for Tips, Hands-On Activities, Watch Out moments, and Checklists throughout each class.

  • Many pages include prompt templates and tool recommendations you can try immediately with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, or other AI platforms.

  • Use this book alongside live sessions, video demonstrations, and AI tool explorations offered through the FGCU AI Academy.

No installation, no coding, and no prior technical knowledge is required to use this book or to participate in the program.

Human-Centered AI#

This program is built on a fundamental belief:

AI is not here to replace people. It is here to amplify what people can think, create, and accomplish.

Every class in this program keeps the human being at the center of the conversation. You will learn that AI works best when paired with your own judgment, values, lived experience, and critical thinking. The final decisions (professional, creative, or personal) always remain yours.

As you move through these classes, keep reflecting on:

  • How can AI help me do things I already do, but faster, better, or with less stress?

  • When should I trust an AI output and when should I verify, edit, or discard it?

  • What does it mean to use AI responsibly, for myself and for others?

Learning Philosophy and Approach#

This program adopts a demo-first, practice-centered approach to learning. The emphasis is not on memorizing tool names or technical concepts, but on doing, experimenting, reflecting, and building transferable skills.

Key principles guiding this program include:

  • Concepts before features
    Understanding how to do something independently of platform and why a tool behaves as it does makes you a better user of every tool, not just the ones covered here.

  • Verification before action
    AI outputs are starting points, not final answers. Every class reinforces the habit of reviewing and critically evaluating what AI produces.

  • Practice over performance
    The goal is not to impress but to build genuine, lasting confidence through repeated, real-world application.

  • Human judgment always
    Creativity, ethics, context, and accountability are uniquely human. AI supports these, it does not replace them.

Program Roadmap at a Glance#

The program follows a clear and practical progression:

  1. Class 1: Demystifying AI and your first prompt conversation

  2. Class 2: AI for emails, documents, spreadsheets, professional pages, CVs, and cover letters

  3. Class 3: AI for images, presentations, music, avatars, digital invitations, brand identity, and websites

  4. Class 4: Verification, privacy, and building your personal AI toolkit

By the end of the program, you will have practiced AI across the most common use cases in everyday and professional life, and you will leave with a personal action plan to keep growing.

What You Will Take Home#

Across all four classes, participants build a personal AI Starter Pack:

  • Prompt Library: tested prompts for emails, documents, and creative tasks

  • Verification Checklist: a practical framework for reviewing AI outputs before using them

  • AI Tool Map: a plain-language guide to which AI tool to use for which task

  • Responsible Use Guide: clear, practical guidelines for privacy and ethical AI use

  • Personal AI Action Plan: your own commitment to three AI habits you will start this week
